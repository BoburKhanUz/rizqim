Markers: **[EST]** = my estimate or calculation; **[VENDOR]** = vendor's own page; **[UNVERIFIED]** = search snippet or secondary source not opened; **[OLD]** = before 2024.

Exchange rate used for conversions: **1 USD = 11,808.76 UZS** (CBU official rate from 01.10.2026), per search result citing the CBU archive — [cbu.uz archive](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/print/) [UNVERIFIED: page not opened; an anhor.uz item cites 12,053 for an unspecified "28 October"]. ## 1. How many catering outlets exist (Uzbekistan, Tashkent, by format, YaTT vs MChJ) and how fast is the count growing?

### Takeaway
There are about **25–26 thousand registered catering legal entities** (stat.uz 25,840 on 1.09.2025; Tax Committee "over 25 thousand"), of which about **20 thousand actually paid tax** in Jan–Aug 2025; Tashkent city holds ~22% of accommodation-and-food businesses but only ~16% of statistical catering output. YaTT counts and a choyxona category are not published anywhere; the working range stays **8–15 thousand choyxona/osh-markazi-type outlets** nationally, ~800–1,350 real choyxonas in Tashkent city (prior estimate). Entry is growing ~7%/yr and nominal turnover ~13%/yr.

### Cited Findings
**Counts of entities**
- 25,840 commercial catering enterprises on 1.09.2025; Jan–Aug 2025 turnover ~133 trln UZS, +12.8% y/y. — [stat.uz, 24.09.2025](https://stat.uz/en/press-center/news-of-committee/64144-umumij-ov-atlanish-korkhonalari-133-trln-s-mlik-savdo-ilgan-4)
- 25,887 commercial catering enterprises on 1.11.2025 (search summary of stat.uz release). — [stat.uz series](https://stat.uz/en/press-center/news-of-committee/65351-restoranlar-10-ojda-ancha-savdo-ilgan-4) [UNVERIFIED]
- Tax Committee: **over 25 thousand** enterprises are registered as legal entities providing catering; in **10 months of 2025** they had **20 trln UZS** of turnover, paid **1.4 trln UZS** of taxes and received 4.6 bn UZS of tax benefits. — [UNDP, "Drivers of informality in the food service sector", May 2026, p.38](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)
- **20,163 legal entities** in public catering paid ~1.1 trln UZS of taxes in Jan–Aug 2025; the top 20 paid 213.3 bn (19.4%). — [Anhor.uz citing Tax Committee](https://anhor.uz/uzl/news/ommaviy-ovqatlanish-korxonalari-soliq/) (via search summary) [UNVERIFIED]; top-20 figures confirmed by [Spot.uz, 20.10.2025](https://www.spot.uz/oz/2025/10/20/catering-tax/)
- Full year 2025: catering paid **over 1.7 trln UZS** in taxes; top 20 paid 335.5 bn (19.6%) on 4.7 trln turnover (KFC franchisee 89.5 bn, Evos 69 bn, Uzbekistan Airways catering 40.4 bn, Oqtepa 38.7 bn). — [Spot.uz, 12.03.2026](https://www.spot.uz/oz/2026/03/12/catering-tax/)
- Statistical catering services volume **2024: 174.9 trln UZS**; by region Tashkent city 27.4 trln (15.6%), Samarqand 19.4 (11.1%), Farg'ona 17.9 (10.2%), Namangan 17.7 (10.1%), Qashqadaryo 16.5 (9.4%), Andijon 12.2 (7.0%), Toshkent viloyati 11.9 (6.8%), Qoraqalpog'iston 10.5 (6.0%), Xorazm 10.1 (5.8%), Buxoro 9.1 (5.2%), Surxondaryo 7.9 (4.5%), Jizzax 5.5, Navoiy 5.4, Sirdaryo 3.5. — [UNDP 2026, Table 1, source: National Statistics Committee](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)
- "Accommodation and food" businesses on 1.03.2025: 27,166, of which Tashkent city 5,975 (22%), Samarqand 3,117, Toshkent viloyati 2,902, Farg'ona 1,950, Buxoro 1,942. — [surxonstat.uz, 17.03.2025](https://www.surxonstat.uz/uz/matbuot-markazi/qo-mita-yangiliklar/13716-17-03-2025-09-02)
- Tashkent city: ~5.7 thousand enterprises in the sector on 1.01.2025, ~5.6 thousand of them small businesses (search snippet). — [toshstat.uz PDF](https://toshstat.uz/2025/Ichki%20savdo/ichki5.pdf) [UNVERIFIED]

**By format (only survey shares exist)**
- UNDP sample of 420 owners in 14 regional centres: **45% kafe/oshxona, 29% restaurant, 11% fast food, 10% choyxona, 2% to'yxona, 1% coffee shop**. 33% operate 1–3 years, 43% 4–10 years, 24% >10 years. 64% have up to 100 seats; average seats: to'yxona 384, restaurant 187, kafe 94, **choyxona 89**, fast food 61. — [UNDP 2026, pp.40–41](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)

**YaTT vs MChJ**
- UNDP reports tax burden by legal form (MChJ 15.7%, YaTT 14%, joint ventures 10%) but not the share of each form in the sample or the population. — [UNDP 2026, p.45](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)

**Growth**
- New accommodation-and-food enterprises, Jan–Apr: 2022 1,834; 2023 1,879; 2024 2,204; 2025 2,361 (+7.1%). — [stat.uz, 21.05.2025](https://stat.uz/en/press-center/news-of-committee/61806-yashash-va-ovqatlanish-sohasida-yangi-tashkil-etilgan-korxonalar-soni-ko-paymoqda-s-3)
- Tax paid by catering: Jan–Apr 2025 >528 bn; Jan–Aug 2025 ~1.1 trln; full 2025 >1.7 trln UZS. — [Spot.uz, 17.06.2025](https://www.spot.uz/oz/2025/06/17/catering-tax/); [Spot.uz, 12.03.2026](https://www.spot.uz/oz/2026/03/12/catering-tax/)

### Inferences
- **Declared vs statistical volume** [EST]: legal entities declared ~20 trln UZS in 10 months 2025 (~24 trln annualised) while the statistical catering volume was 174.9 trln for 2024. Even allowing for definitional differences, **legal entities' declared turnover covers only ~15% of statistical catering output**; the rest is YaTT, unregistered outlets and under-reporting. That is the unautomated mass Rizqim targets, and also the reason payment capacity per registered entity looks low.
- **Average declared turnover per legal entity** [EST]: 24 trln / ~25 thousand ≈ **~0.96 bn UZS/yr ≈ 80 mln UZS/month ≈ 6,800 USD/month**. Median is far lower, because the top 20 alone declared 4.7 trln (≈20%).
- **Only ~20.2 of ~25.9 thousand registered legal entities paid any tax** in Jan–Aug 2025 [EST, from two sources]: ~20% look dormant or zero-tax. Use ~20 thousand as the "active registered" base.
- **Outlet universe** [EST, prior note]: 26 thousand legal entities × 1.3–1.8 (YaTT, informal, branches) ≈ **34–47 thousand outlets**. Choyxona + osh markazi ≈ 8–15 thousand; core paying segment (independent urban choyxonas) ≈ 3–6 thousand.
- **Tashkent** [EST]: ~5.7–6.0 thousand registered businesses; 15.6% of statistical volume vs 22% of businesses, so Tashkent firms are not larger per firm in reported volume — volume is spread widely in Farg'ona valley and Samarqand too (valley = 27% of volume but only 16% of registered businesses, consistent with heavy informality there).
- **Revenue pool at 20 USD/month** [EST]:
  - All ~20 thousand active legal entities × 240 USD = **~4.8 mln USD/yr** theoretical ceiling for one-site SaaS at that price; ~34–47 thousand outlets × 240 = 8–11 mln USD/yr.
  - Unautomated legal entities (64% of 20–26 thousand) ≈ 13–16.6 thousand → **3.1–4.0 mln USD/yr**.
  - Choyxona core segment 3–6 thousand × 240 = **0.7–1.4 mln USD/yr**.
  - Existing spend: ~36% of ~25 thousand ≈ 9 thousand firms already pay for software; at a typical 150–650 thousand UZS/month (13–55 USD), current POS SaaS spend is roughly **1.4–6 mln USD/yr**.
  - A realistic 3-year share of 3–5% of the choyxona core = 90–300 outlets = **22–72 thousand USD ARR** at 20 USD/month. The business case needs expansion into kafe/oshxona (45% of the market by count) and multi-outlet add-ons.

### Gaps
- No official count of YaTT in catering (OKED 56.x) and no MChJ/YaTT split; neither soliq.uz nor stat.uz publishes it. siat.stat.uz tables by OKED 56.10/56.30 were not downloaded.
- No official choyxona / osh markazi / kafe counts; only UNDP sample shares and 2GIS counts.
- 2026 counts of catering enterprises (after Nov 2025) not found.
- Daryo (Feb 2026) cited 26,390 "accommodation and food" entities with unclear date; page returned 403 (prior note).

## 2. What share already uses management software and fiscal devices, and what pushes adoption (VM 393, fines)?

### Takeaway
UNDP (survey 2025, published May 2026): **97%** have an official cash register, but only **36%** have R-Keeper-type management software and **73%** of those are tax-integrated (≈26% of all firms). From **22.10.2026** VM Resolution 393 bans catering firms from using accounting or order software that is not integrated with tax systems, and the integration fine is **2% of last quarter's net revenue (20% if repeated)**. That makes integrated software a compliance purchase, not a nice-to-have.

### Cited Findings
- 97% of catering firms have official cash registers; 94% use them constantly, 6% report occasional breakdowns. Only **36%** have "R-Keeper or similar" management software; **73%** of those are integrated with the tax base. 89% say they always issue receipts, but **32%** do not always issue one for cash payments. — [UNDP 2026, p.44](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)
- UNDP recommendations: broadly incentivise tax-integrated management software for catering; subsidies for micro-firms during adaptation, a phased schedule without fines, and a technical-help line, because "many small catering firms cannot adopt new systems without help" (staff lack technical capacity and time). It also proposes giving the **VAT cashback** (currently 40% of VAT paid if cashless ≥60%, 20% if <60%, VMQ-745 of 07.11.2024) **only to firms with tax-integrated software**, after a ≥12-month transition. — [UNDP 2026, pp.63–64](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)
- VM 393 (20.07.2026, in force for these clauses from **22.10.2026**): catering firms may not use accounting or order-tracking software not integrated with tax-authority systems; payments by the bank's unified QR also require a receipt. — [lex.uz, VM 393](https://lex.uz/docs/-8343102) (from `fiskal_background.md`, marked confirmed there)
- Fines (Tax Code, current text): selling without a till / no receipt / refusing card or QR — 5 mln UZS (Art. 221-1); unregistered till 7 mln; someone else's terminal or QR 20 mln; non-compliant till 20 mln; wrong MXIK 1% of sale; breach of **tax-system integration** or fiscal-sign rules — **2% of last quarter's net revenue, 20% if repeated within a year** (Art. 227¹, as amended 29.05.2024). In 2023–24 this fine equalled the whole quarter's revenue and restaurants were fined hundreds of billions for non-integrated R-Keeper/iiko/Jowi. — [lex.uz Tax Code](https://lex.uz/docs/-4674902); [buxgalter.uz, 06.12.2023](https://buxgalter.uz/publish/doc/text194910_pismo_nalogovyh_organov_o_shtrafah_za_to_chto_net_integracii_programm_roznichnoy_torgovli_i_obshchepita_s_sistemami_nalogovyh_organov_v_uzbekistane) [OLD] (from `fiskal_background.md`)
- Fiscal module / virtual fiscal module operator fee: 1 BHM per year (BHM 412,000 UZS from 1.08.2025; 440,000 from 1.09.2026). — [goldenpages.uz](https://www.goldenpages.uz/zarplata/) [UNVERIFIED secondary]; VM 943 para 55 per `fiskal_background.md`
- Customers pay by card 59% and cash 45% on average (multi-answer); **45% of choyxonas** and 38% of to'yxonas offer card-to-card transfer instead of terminal/cash, the main way to hide turnover. — [UNDP 2026, p.44](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)
- Since 13.05.2026 the Tax Committee analyses P2P receipts of outlet owners (prior note `tolov_fiskal_huquq.md`).
- Only 25% of entrepreneurs use digital payment systems (CERR + Chamber survey of >5,000 entrepreneurs); ~108 thousand business QR codes issued in 2025. — [uzbekembassy.in, 21.08.2026](https://www.uzbekembassy.in/?p=37573); [UzDaily](https://www.uzdaily.uz/en/uzbekistan-is-building-digital-payment-infrastructure-can-it-become-sme-finance-infrastructure/) (from report section 7)

### Inferences
- **Software adoption** [EST]: 36% × ~25 thousand ≈ 9 thousand firms with software; ≈ 6.6 thousand tax-integrated; **~16 thousand legal entities with no management software**. The UNDP sample is 14 regional centres only, averaging 135 guests/day, so adoption in district towns and among YaTT is probably lower.
- **Fine as a sales argument** [EST]: for a choyxona at UNDP's 7.1 mln UZS/day (~213 mln/month, ~640 mln/quarter), a 2% integration fine ≈ **12.8 mln UZS ≈ 1,080 USD**, about **4.5 years of a 20 USD/month subscription**; a repeat (20%) ≈ 128 mln UZS. Even a small outlet at 1 mln UZS/day faces ~1.8 mln UZS (≈ 150 USD) per offence.
- The proposed VAT-cashback-only-for-integrated-software rule (if adopted) would add a direct money incentive for MChJ above the 1 bn threshold; YaTT on 1% turnover tax get no such benefit. Status of adoption unknown.

### Gaps
- No data on how VM 393 is enforced after 22.10.2026 (inspections, grace period). No post-October 2026 news found.
- No breakdown of the 36% by format (choyxona vs restaurant) or by region in the UNDP report.
- Exact definition of "integrated" (own fiscal registration vs via a registered virtual kassa) remains open (see `fiskal_background.md`).

## 3. What do small businesses in Uzbekistan pay for other SaaS, monthly vs annual, how they pay, and how dealers/agents sell?

### Takeaway
Local SME SaaS lists prices **monthly in UZS** at roughly **70k–1.4 mln UZS/month (≈6–115 USD)**, with entry plans of **125k–350k UZS (≈10–30 USD)**, and gives **10–20% off for annual prepay**. Cloud POS vendors invoice and accept bank transfer, card and cash; Click/Payme recurring billing was not found on any vendor page. Dealer and referral programmes exist (Paloma365, MoySklad, iiko/R-Keeper integrators), but no POS vendor publishes commission rates; the only public Uzbek SaaS partner rate found is **10–15% of the client's recurring spend** (Servercore cloud).

### Cited Findings
**Non-catering SME SaaS (new)**
- **BILLZ** (retail POS, "more than 7,000 stores"): Start **299,000 UZS/month** (up to 10 users, 3 locations), Advanced **499,000** (20 users, 5 locations), Pro **999,000**; extra location **+179,000**; **20% discount for paying a year or more**; 7-day free trial; managers do product entry, hardware setup and training free; >10 locations negotiable. Payment methods not stated. — [billz.io/prices](https://billz.io/prices) [VENDOR], fetched 24.10.2026
- **MoySklad Uzbekistan** (inventory/retail SaaS; Tashkent office since 2023): Free (0, up to 5 users); Start **69,300 UZS/month, annual billing only**; Basic **196,000 monthly or 156,800 on annual**; Prof **574,000 / 459,200**; Corp **1,372,000 / 1,097,600**. Discounts for prepaying 3/6/12 months (5%/10%/20%). Prices raised from **1.07.2026**. — [moysklad.uz/subscription](https://www.moysklad.uz/subscription/) [VENDOR]; [moysklad.ru news, 29.05.2026](https://www.moysklad.ru/news/novye-tarify-s-1-iyulya-2026/)
- **Smartup** (smartup24.com) is a distribution / sales-rep platform, not a restaurant POS; its pricing page was not reached (redirect from smartup.uz). — [smartup.uz → smartup24.com](https://smartup24.com/)

**Catering POS (from prior notes, for the same table)**
- Paloma365 **134,700 / 224,500 / 269,400 UZS/month** per outlet; annual payment saves ~10%; 15-day trial; free training and setup; unlimited users. **Payment: bank transfer, card, cash and payment terminals; invoices are issued; billing on the 1st of each month.** Four partner tracks (referral, reseller, integrator, other), commission not published. — [paloma365.uz/tariffs](https://paloma365.uz/tariffs) [VENDOR], fetched 24.10.2026

- JOWi from 29.99 USD/month (2019) [OLD]. — [weproject.media](https://www.weproject.media/)

**Dealer / partner economics**
- Servercore (cloud) partner programme in Uzbekistan: **10% of the client's monthly spend, 15% above 50 mln UZS**, paid for as long as the client stays (Nov 2023). — [the-tech.kz](https://the-tech.kz/oblachnyj-provajder-servercore-zapustil-partnerskuyu-programmu-v-uzbekistane/) [OLD-ish, VENDOR]
- Kapitalbank pays partners commission for referrals through Uzum Business; rate not stated. — [Spot.uz, 27.05.2024](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2024/05/27/kapitalbank/) [UNVERIFIED snippet]

**Cash culture of the buyers (how they pay suppliers)**
- Rent: 41% pay in cash, 40% by bank transfer, 19% mixed. Food purchases: 58% cash, 33% bank transfer; **76% of those buying at bazaars pay cash**. Wages: 41% cash, 35% card, 24% mixed. Tax reports: 62% have in-house accountant, 25% outsourced, 13% do it themselves. — [UNDP 2026, pp.42–45](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Price anchors** [EST]: 20 USD ≈ 236,000 UZS sits **in the middle of the local entry band** — above MoySklad Basic annual (156,800) and Paloma365 Starter (134,700), close to RAOS Starter (249,000), below BILLZ Start (299,000) and Biznex Standard (349,000), and well below iiko (54 USD). It is not a discount price; it is market-rate. Pricing it **in UZS** (e.g. 199,000 or 229,000 UZS) matches every local competitor; USD pricing is used only by iiko/BROS/Poster.
- **Annual prepay** [EST]: every vendor that shows a discount gives 10–20% for annual; MoySklad's cheapest plan is annual-only. So annual billing is normal in the market, but **the default list price is monthly** and annual is an upsell. Asking ~2.83 mln UZS up front (≈2 weeks of a cashier's wage, ~40% of a choyxona's average daily revenue) is feasible for established venues but a hurdle for small ones; offer monthly at +15–20% and annual as the discounted option.
- **Payment channel** [EST]: given 41–58% cash payment for rent, food and wages, expect many owners to pay by **cash or card to an agent/dealer** or by **invoice + bank transfer (MChJ needs an invoice for expense deduction)**. Click/Payme card-on-file recurring would suit YaTT. None of the competitors advertise Click/Payme auto-renewal; it is unconfirmed whether it is common.
- **Dealer model** [EST]: competitors rely on free onboarding (menu entry, training) done by staff or partners. A dealer commission of 10–20% recurring (or first-year 20–30%) is consistent with the only published Uzbek rate (Servercore 10–15%) but POS-specific rates are not public.

### Gaps
- No POS/SaaS vendor's dealer commission rate in Uzbekistan was found; must be asked directly (Paloma365 +998 71 205-05-26, Rahmat, iiko integrators).
- No survey on monthly vs annual preference or on churn of SMB SaaS in Uzbekistan.
- No public data on whether BILLZ/Paloma365 bill through Click/Payme subscriptions.
- 1C (Uzbek localisation) licence/support prices, Uzum/Yandex seller-tool fees, CRM (amoCRM/Bitrix24 UZ) prices were not collected in this pass.

## 4. What does a choyxona/kafe earn per month, and what is 20 USD as a share of revenue and of wages?

### Takeaway
UNDP's 2025 owner survey gives **average daily revenue of 7.1 mln UZS for a choyxona** (≈213 mln UZS ≈ 18,000 USD a month) and 10 mln for kafe/oshxona; 20 USD/month is about **0.1% of a typical surveyed choyxona's revenue**, and still under 1% for a small venue doing 1 mln UZS/day. Against labour costs it is ~17% of one minimum wage (1.36 mln UZS from 1.09.2026) and a few percent of a cashier's pay, so affordability is not the binding constraint; trust and habit are.

### Cited Findings
- Average **daily revenue**: kafe/oshxona **10 mln UZS**, restaurant **17.9 mln**, fast food **7.5 mln**, **choyxona 7.1 mln**, coffee shop 4.8 mln, to'yxona 21.9 mln. Daily guests: overall 135; kafe 128, restaurant 173, fast food 146, **choyxona 105**, coffee 80. Average spend per guest: overall 75k; kafe 67k, restaurant 99k, fast food 48k, **choyxona 67k**, coffee 56k, to'yxona 94k UZS. — [UNDP 2026, pp.41–42](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf) (survey of 420 owners in 14 regional centres; report published May 2026)
- Customers report average spend of 81k UZS per visit (low-income 63k, middle 77k, high 110k); customers' average income 6.9 mln UZS. — [UNDP 2026, p.~52](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)
- 2GIS average bill for "Чайхана": Tashkent 40,000–250,000; Samarqand 35,000–300,000; Buxoro 50,000–200,000 UZS (21.10.2026). — [2GIS Tashkent](https://2gis.uz/tashkent/search/%D0%A7%D0%B0%D0%B9%D1%85%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B0) (prior note)
- Average tax burden reported by owners: 15.9% (choyxona 16%); Tax Committee data give 7.2% of turnover. — [UNDP 2026, p.45](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)
- Minimum wage (MROT): 1,050,000 UZS (from 1.12.2023), 1,155,000 (1.10.2024), 1,271,000 (1.08.2025), **1,360,000 (1.09.2026)**; BHM 412,000 → **440,000** from 1.09.2026. — [goldenpages.uz](https://www.goldenpages.uz/zarplata/) [UNVERIFIED secondary; dates contain small inconsistencies; check lex.uz]
- Average nominal wage: 6.82 mln UZS in Q1 2026 (Tashkent city 11.684 mln); **7.09 mln** in Jan–Jun 2026 (+18.4% y/y). — [kun.uz, 28.04.2026](https://kun.uz/ru/news/2026/04/28/srednyaya-zarplata-v-uzbekistane-dostigla-68-mln-sumov); [anhor.uz, 26.07.2026](https://anhor.uz/news/zarplaty-v-uzbekistane-po-otraslyam-2026/)
- UNDP uses a catering employee earning **3 mln UZS/month** as its example (annual taxes 8.6 mln UZS ≈ 683 USD). ### Inferences
- **Monthly revenue ranges** [EST]:
  - UNDP choyxona: 7.1 mln × 30 ≈ **213 mln UZS/month ≈ 18,000 USD** (≈ 2.6 bn UZS/yr, i.e. above the 1 bn turnover-tax threshold, so these respondents are skewed to larger, VAT-paying regional-centre venues or overstate).
  - Declared-turnover average per legal entity ≈ 80 mln UZS/month (Q1 inference).
  - A small mahalla choyxona (e.g. 30–50 guests × 40–60k) ≈ 1.5–3 mln/day ≈ 45–90 mln UZS/month [EST, not sourced].
  - So the working range is **~45–300 mln UZS/month (≈ 4,000–25,000 USD)** for paying-capable venues.
- **20 USD (236k UZS) as % of revenue** [EST]: 0.08% at 300 mln; **0.11% at 213 mln (UNDP choyxona)**; 0.3% at 80 mln; 0.5% at 45 mln. Compare: typical acquiring fee ~1–2% of card sales [UNVERIFIED general knowledge], owner-reported tax burden 16%.
- **20 USD vs labour** [EST]: 17% of one minimum wage; ~8% of a 3 mln catering wage; 3–5% of a 4.5–7 mln Tashkent cashier. If the POS saves one cashier-hour a day or stops one waiter's unrecorded bill a month (a few hundred thousand UZS), it pays back.
- **Conclusion on 20 USD/month, annual** [EST]: price is affordable and in line with the market for urban choyxonas and kafes; the main risk is not the amount but (a) **up-front annual payment** (2.83 mln UZS) for small/cash-run venues and (b) proving value before the money is paid. Monthly billing in UZS with an annual discount, plus a free trial and free onboarding (what Paloma365/BILLZ/RAOS already give), is the market norm.

## 5. What blocks adoption: trust, internet/electricity outages, one-time-purchase preference, piracy?

### Takeaway
The documented barriers are **unreliable internet (26% of firms cite it as a reason for not issuing receipts), till breakdowns (20%), bank-link drops (11%)**, cost of tills and reporting systems (24% name it a barrier to formalisation), low staff tech capacity, and **frequent power cuts** (up to 8–10 unannounced outages a day at some venues in summer 2025; >18 thousand outage cases in Tashkent in one summer-2026 month). Unlicensed-software use now carries criminal liability (since Nov 2025), which weakens the pirated-desktop alternative. No source quantified preference for one-time purchase over subscription.

### Cited Findings
- Reasons for not issuing receipts: 26% poor internet; 20% till works badly; 14% customer doesn't need it; 11% bank connection drops; 8% takes time / frequent breakdowns; 50% report no problem. — [UNDP 2026, pp.44–45](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)
- Financial barriers to formalisation: 62% tax burden, **24% cost of cash-register equipment and reporting systems**, 15% licences, 10% bank fees (POS, QR, account). — [UNDP 2026, "V. Formalisation", item 15](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf) (from `raqobatchilar.md`)
- Accounting staff: 30% of owners cite a lack of qualified people, 20% cost, 9% incomprehensible tax reports. — [UNDP 2026, p.45](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)
- 11% of owners met corruption in the last 12 months; 10% of those with regional electricity networks. — [UNDP 2026, p.48](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)
- Power cuts, Chamber of Commerce dialogue (14.07.2025): cafés report up to **10 unannounced outages a day**, another operator 8 a day; owners cannot reach the grid company, which has not accepted responsibility; café owners ask for compensation. — [Spot.uz, 14.07.2025](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2025/07/14/catering-blackout/)
- Summer 2026: svetanet.uz logged **>18 thousand outage cases in Tashkent in a month** (worst: Chilanzar, Mirzo-Ulugbek, Almazar); Ministry of Energy said in Oct 2026 no winter 2026/27 shortages are expected apart from planned repairs. — [Spot.uz, 01.08.2026](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/08/01/city-of-lights/); [Spot.uz, 16.10.2026](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/10/16/winter-is-coming/) (via search summaries) [UNVERIFIED]
- Local POS marketing leads with offline mode (Biznex: "'If the internet goes down, sales stop' — a phrase heard every day"; RAOS stresses its offline capability). — [biznex.uz](https://www.biznex.uz/); [raos.uz](https://raos.uz/kassa/kafe) [VENDOR] (from `raqobatchilar.md`)
- Piracy: copyright amendments (ZRU-1080) introduced **criminal liability for unlicensed software use (Criminal Code Art. 149-1) from November 2025**, up to 3 years' imprisonment; no Uzbekistan-specific piracy rate is published (latest global BSA estimate 37%, 2018). — [beeline.uz](https://beeline.uz/en/events/news/piratskoe-po-v-korporativnom-segmente) [VENDOR commentary, UNVERIFIED]
- 7% of owners avoided investing in their venue for fear of having to pay bribes for permits. — [UNDP 2026, p.48](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-05/ga_report_uzb.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Offline-first is mandatory**, not optional: a cloud-only POS fails on the two most cited operational problems (internet, power). A tablet/phone POS with battery and offline queue is an advantage over desktop monoblocks that die with the power.
- **Trust/visibility trade-off** [EST]: tax-integrated software makes turnover visible; 45% of choyxonas steer customers to P2P and 32% of firms skip cash receipts, so some owners will resist a POS precisely because it records everything. The VM 393 ban and 2% fine flip this: the risk now sits on *not* having integrated software.
- **One-time purchase / piracy** [EST]: cracked desktop R-Keeper/1C and one-off Telegram bot builds (OLX) are the "free" alternatives; criminal liability since Nov 2025 and the need for tax integration (which pirated builds cannot keep updated) reduce this option. A "hardware + first year" bundle could satisfy owners who prefer one-off payments.

### Gaps

- No Uzbekistan-specific software piracy rate.
- No data on outage frequency outside Tashkent or on generator ownership in catering.
- No independent reviews/complaints data about local POS vendors (trust signals).
