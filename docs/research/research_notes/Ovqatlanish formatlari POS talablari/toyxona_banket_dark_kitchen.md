# To'yxona / banket zallari and delivery-only kitchens in Uzbekistan (2023–2026 practice, POS/booking input)

Research date: 2026-10-21. About 30 searches and page fetches, in Uzbek, Russian and English. Each fact below has a source next to it. Anything without a source is under "Inferences" or "Gaps". Public sources say little about how halls and kitchens run day to day: deposit percentages, how guest-count changes are handled, calendar tools, kitchen planning. Most of that has to be confirmed in interviews (see the Gaps sections).

## 1. How are to'y/banket bookings priced and paid, and what happens when guest count changes?

### Takeaway
Pricing is **per guest (kishi boshiga)** from a fixed menu tier. Some venues add a **separate hall-rent line** and other extras. Typical Tashkent ranges in 2023–2026 are **~250,000 to 900,000+ UZS per guest** in to'yxonas, with mid-range venues at **390–500k UZS**, and hotels up to **~1.95M UZS**. Venues also set a **minimum guest count** (often 100). A **deposit (avans/zakalat) is standard**, but I found no reliable public source for the usual percentage, the payment schedule, the rules for changing guest count, or cancellation and refund terms. These are agreed venue by venue in a contract, and media advise couples to check them in writing.

### Cited Findings

**Per-guest prices (UZS / USD)**
- 2023, Tashkent to'yxonas: average starts at **250,000 so'm per person** and tops out at **900,000 so'm**. Newly renovated venues start at **600,000 so'm**. The average wedding is **~200 guests**. A venue whose renovated interior differs from its usual look may charge extra beyond the menu — [Kursiv UZ, 22.08.2023](https://uz.kursiv.media/uz/2023-08-22/serxarajat-sentyabr-toshkentda-ortacha-toyga-qancha-sarflash-kerak/) *(2023 data)*
- 2023, hotel halls: built before independence **370–450k**, after independence **750–850k**, recently built **950k–1.95M so'm per guest**. Star hotels charge **30–45M so'm hall rent on top of the menu**, while older hotels usually don't charge rent separately. Hotel halls typically hold 100–330 guests, and one holds ~600 — [Kursiv UZ, 2023](https://uz.kursiv.media/uz/2023-08-22/serxarajat-sentyabr-toshkentda-ortacha-toyga-qancha-sarflash-kerak/)
- 2025: the average banquet check at "Turkiston" restaurant (Tashkent) is **$30–50 per guest**, so 200 guests cost $6,000–10,000. An average Tashkent wedding of 150–200 guests costs $5,000–10,000, and venue type is the biggest price driver — [Kursiv, 29.04.2025](https://uz.kursiv.media/2025-04-29/ekonomika-svadeb-v-tashkente-skolko-stoit-torzhestvo-i-kto-na-etom-zarabatyvaet-v-2025-godu/)
- Undated (likely 2024–25): a basic banquet (hot dish and salads) starts at **~250,000 so'm per person** — [Podrobno.uz](https://podrobno.uz/cat/obchestvo/vo-skolko-oboydetsya-svadba-v-tashkente-v-etom-sezone-rasskazyvaem-/)
- Versal (Tashkent) is "from 800,000 so'm per person", with no detail on what is included — [ANONS.uz digest, via search snippet](https://anons.uz/ru/digest/gde-provesti-svadbu-v-tashkente)
- 2026 price list example, Seoul Banquet Hall (Tashkent): **Menu 1 from 390,000 UZS/guest** (70+ dishes, European and Korean), **Menu 2 450,000 UZS/guest**, **hall rent "from 25,000,000 UZS"**, **minimum 100 guests**, main hall 100–320 guests. Deposit, service %, corkage and cancellation are not stated. Bookings come by phone, email, web form, Telegram, WhatsApp and Instagram — [seoulhall.uz](https://seoulhall.uz/)
- 2026, Afisha budget model: assumes **500,000 so'm per guest** "including hall and basic service". Banquet totals are 25M (50 guests), 50M (100) and 100M (200). Full wedding totals are ~94M / ~129M / ~198M so'm — [Afisha.uz, 24.08.2026 (UZ)](https://www.afisha.uz/uz/weddings/2026/08/24/toy-xarajatlari); [RU version](https://www.afisha.uz/ru/weddings/2026/08/24/wedding-budget)

**Extra charges / line items**
- Afisha warns that **rent, service, textiles, equipment, hall extension, serving food brought from outside, and seating for vendors (photographer, host, musicians)** may be billed separately. A 400k menu with these extras can cost more than an all-inclusive 500k menu — [Afisha.uz 2026](https://www.afisha.uz/uz/weddings/2026/08/24/toy-xarajatlari)
- A toastmaster advises that **lighting and music should be included in the hall rent** and not sold separately, which implies some venues do charge for them separately — [Kursiv 2025](https://uz.kursiv.media/2025-04-29/ekonomika-svadeb-v-tashkente-skolko-stoit-torzhestvo-i-kto-na-etom-zarabatyvaet-v-2025-godu/)
- Third-party services the client usually pays for separately (2025): toastmaster $200–700 (stars $1,000+), musicians $300–500 per evening, star singers $1,500–2,500 per set, decor/florist $200–500, car convoy + photo + video ~$800–1,000 — [Kursiv 2025](https://uz.kursiv.media/2025-04-29/ekonomika-svadeb-v-tashkente-skolko-stoit-torzhestvo-i-kto-na-etom-zarabatyvaet-v-2025-godu/). Cake costs ~180–200k so'm/kg — [Afisha 2026](https://www.afisha.uz/uz/weddings/2026/08/24/toy-xarajatlari)
- A restaurant banquet page (MANANA, Tashkent) offers decor (flowers, candles, textiles, photo zones, banners) as an add-on and composes the menu individually. It publishes no prices or deposit terms — [manana.uz/banquet](https://manana.uz/banquet)

**Deposit / payment / contract**
- Afisha (2026) says the restaurant "usually asks for a deposit" but gives no amount. It advises getting the full total before paying the deposit and keeping one document per purchase listing **full price, deposit, remaining balance, next payment date, what is included, and whether cancellation is refundable** — [Afisha.uz 2026](https://www.afisha.uz/uz/weddings/2026/08/24/toy-xarajatlari)
- A web-studio blog claims a hall booking form typically takes "**a deposit of 5–15M so'm upfront**" and that halls are booked **6–12 months ahead**. This is a vendor marketing blog, so it is low reliability — [sayt.uz blog](https://sayt.uz/en/blog/nikoh-toyxona-sayt)
- Searches in Uzbek ("to'yxona avans zakalat… qaytariladimi", "pulni qaytarmadi") and Russian ("предоплата банкет Ташкент задаток") returned **no** Uzbek source on deposit percentage, refund rules or court cases.

**Guest-count changes**
- No venue policy found. Afisha notes only that fewer guests reduce the banquet cost, and that small weddings may need another venue because halls usually require ≥100 guests — [Afisha 2026](https://www.afisha.uz/uz/weddings/2026/08/24/toy-xarajatlari); [seoulhall.uz minimum 100](https://seoulhall.uz/)

**Regulation that directly limits guest count and hours (affects booking validation)**
- The regulation on weddings and family events was approved by a joint decision of the Senate and Legislative Chamber councils on **14.09.2019** and took effect **01.01.2020**. Kun.uz (Apr 2025) says it **is still in force**. Limits: **family events/weddings up to 200 guests; related osh (nahorgi osh) up to 250; double weddings up to 250; double-wedding osh up to 300**. Events must be **one day, between 06:00 and 23:00**. The mahalla must be notified **≥1 week before**. No more than 3 cars. **Licensed venues/cafés must provide a copy of their license.** Fines: citizens **10 BCV (3.75M so'm)**, **venue officials 30 BCV, repeat 50 BCV** — [Kun.uz, 22.04.2025](https://kun.uz/news/2025/04/22/ozbekistonda-toy-otkazish-tartibi-yangilanadi)
- A presidential decree of **21.04.2025** ordered a **new joint decision regulating weddings by August 2025**. As of the Kun.uz article it was pending, and a Daryo summary (via search snippet) said it had not yet been published — [Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/news/2025/04/22/ozbekistonda-toy-otkazish-tartibi-yangilanadi); [Kursiv 2025](https://uz.kursiv.media/2025-04-29/ekonomika-svadeb-v-tashkente-skolko-stoit-torzhestvo-i-kto-na-etom-zarabatyvaet-v-2025-godu/). **STATUS UNCLEAR as of Oct 2026.** I could not confirm whether the new joint decision has been adopted or whether the limits changed.
- Cabinet of Ministers resolution **No. 589 of 16.09.2025** puts weddings, ceremonies and family events under control within the **"Save Food" (Oqilona iste'mol)** program. It restates the 200/250/250/300 guest limits and bans customs such as "pul tarqatish" and "mato ulashish" — [UzA](https://uza.uz/ar/posts/toy-va-marosimlar-otkazilishi-qatiy-nazoratga-olinadi_762965). One Zamin.uz article dates it 2024, while UzA and Daryo say 2025. The full text, including any hall obligations or food-waste reporting, was not verified on lex.uz.

### Inferences
- The POS needs a **booking/event entity**: date, time slot (morning osh / evening banquet), hall, menu tier × guest count, hall-rent line, extras lines, a **deposit ledger** (multiple partial payments with dates), and a final settlement, plus a printable/shareable contract or summary. Afisha's checklist (full price, deposit, balance, next payment date, inclusions, cancellation terms) works as a minimum field list.
- **Guest-count validation against legal caps** (200 / 250 osh / 250 double / 300 double osh, 06:00–23:00) is a sensible soft warning, since venue officials face fines of 30–50 BCV. Make the caps configurable because the rules may change.
- Mixed currency is likely: media quote both USD ($30–50/guest) and UZS. Settlement should be in UZS, possibly with a USD reference.
- Per-guest pricing plus a minimum guest count plus a separate hall rent suggests the bill formula is: max(guests, min_guests) × menu_price + hall_rent + extras (+ service % if any). The service % is unverified.

### Gaps
- **Deposit %**, payment timing (e.g., X% at booking, rest on the event day or after), and refund/cancellation rules: no public Uzbek source found. Interview question.
- **How guest-count changes are handled** (deadline for the final count, whether extra guests on the day are billed per head, whether the minimum is enforced): not found.
- **Service charge % (xizmat haqi), waiter fees, corkage for own alcohol (olib kelingan ichimliklar)**: not found in any price list or article. Many to'y are alcohol-free, but policy at restaurants that host korporativ events is unknown.
- **Nahorgi osh pricing** (per qozon or per guest) was not found in 2023–2026 sources.
- Prices for regions outside Tashkent were not found.
- Current status of the post-April-2025 wedding regulation is unclear.

## 2. What tools do halls use to manage the calendar and avoid double booking? (also capacity, seasonality, kitchen planning)

### Takeaway
I found no public source describing the internal tools to'yxonas use: notebook, Excel, Telegram or CRM. Venues take bookings by **phone, website forms, Telegram, WhatsApp and Instagram**, and plan months ahead around a **spring–autumn season** with a "dead" winter. A large Tashkent restaurant ran **~230–240 weddings a year** and was booked months ahead. Morning and evening events are booked separately.

### Cited Findings
- Seoul Banquet Hall lists booking via phone, email, website request form, **Telegram, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Facebook**, open 10:00–23:00 — [seoulhall.uz](https://seoulhall.uz/). Ribambelle (catering) says it calls back within 15 minutes to "confirm the booking", and MANANA says "contact a manager" — [search snippet](https://ribambelle.uz/katering); [manana.uz](https://manana.uz/banquet)
- "Turkiston" restaurant (Tashkent) held **~230–240 weddings last year**. Its manager calls winter a "dead" period, and the calendar was largely booked until June. Venues take bookings for **morning and evening events** well in advance — [Kursiv 2025](https://uz.kursiv.media/2025-04-29/ekonomika-svadeb-v-tashkente-skolko-stoit-torzhestvo-i-kto-na-etom-zarabatyvaet-v-2025-godu/)
- In 2023, autumn was called the best season, and capital venues' autumn slots were "already booked" by August — [Kursiv UZ 2023](https://uz.kursiv.media/uz/2023-08-22/serxarajat-sentyabr-toshkentda-ortacha-toyga-qancha-sarflash-kerak/)
- Tashkent registered **22,352 marriages in 2023 and 22,755 in 2024** — [Kursiv 2025](https://uz.kursiv.media/2025-04-29/ekonomika-svadeb-v-tashkente-skolko-stoit-torzhestvo-i-kto-na-etom-zarabatyvaet-v-2025-godu/). Nationwide, 296,689 marriages were registered in 2022 — [stat.uz (search snippet)](https://aholi.stat.uz/en/64-news-eng/2323-o-zbekiston-respublikasida-2022-yilda-yoshlar-o-rtasida-qayd-etilgan-nikohlar-2)
- Capacity examples: Yakka Saroy up to 900, Osiyo Grand up to 1,000, Shoh Saroy up to 1,200 guests. A 2GIS catalogue lists ~1,225 "banquet halls for weddings" in Tashkent — [search snippets: weproject.media](https://weproject.media/articles/detail/gde-provesti-torzhestvo-v-tashkente-10-luchshikh-banketnykh-zalov-goroda/), [2GIS](https://2gis.uz/tashkent/search/%D0%91%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%BA%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B9%20%D0%B7%D0%B0%D0%BB%20%D0%B4%D0%BB%D1%8F%20%D1%81%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%B4%D1%8C%D0%B1%D1%8B%20%D0%B2%20%D0%A2%D0%B0%D1%88%D0%BA%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D0%B5) *(snippet-level, not fetched)*. Note that these capacities exceed the 200-guest legal cap for weddings. They likely matter for osh, corporate or other events, or are nominal capacity.
- Event types one hall advertises: national and European weddings, anniversaries, birthdays, corporate events, gala dinners, conferences, buffets, children's parties, national holidays — [seoulhall.uz](https://seoulhall.uz/)
- A web-studio blog claims customers book halls **6–12 months ahead**, that venues take **5–25 new weddings per month** depending on size, and that many venues "hide" availability and make customers phone. Low reliability (marketing content) — [sayt.uz](https://sayt.uz/en/blog/nikoh-toyxona-sayt)
- General CRM usage in Uzbekistan (all industries, by websites): Bitrix24 ~59.5%, amoCRM ~18.9% — [wmtips.com (search snippet)](https://www.wmtips.com/technologies/crm/country/uz/). This is not specific to halls.

### Inferences
- High volume (~230–240 events a year at one restaurant, about 4–5 a week in season), separate morning (osh) and evening slots, and multiple halls per venue mean the calendar unit must be **hall × date × slot**, with a hard conflict check and a "tentative/held without deposit" state that differs from "confirmed with deposit".
- Since enquiries arrive through Telegram, Instagram and phone, a lead/enquiry stage with source tracking is useful before a date is locked.
- A 6–12 month forward horizon, if accurate, means price lists change between booking and event. Bookings should **freeze the agreed per-guest price** at contract time.
- Kitchen production planning from bookings (menu tier × final guest count → ingredient list / zagotovka) is a natural POS feature. No source describes how halls do this today.

### Gaps
- **Actual tools** (paper daftar, Excel, Google Calendar, Telegram groups, Bitrix24/amoCRM, local software) used by to'yxonas: **no source found** in Uzbek or Russian searches. Interview question.
- **Double-booking incidents** or practices to prevent them: no source found.
- **How the kitchen plans production** from bookings (ingredient calculation per guest, purchasing, staff/waiter scheduling): no source found.
- Waiter staffing ratios and how waiters are paid (per event, % of bill): not found.

## 3. How do delivery-only kitchens manage orders from Yandex Eats, Uzum Tezkor, Express24, Wolt, Telegram bots and phone at once?

### Takeaway
The aggregator market has consolidated. **Yandex Eats bought part of Express24's assets (Oct 2024) and controls ~87.5%** of the food-delivery aggregator market. **Uzum Tezkor** was declared dominant in April 2025 (19 cities, 2,400+ partners, ~7,000 courier partners). **Wolt exited Uzbekistan, with no new orders from 5 March 2026.** Express24 launched the first **dark-kitchen hub** in Tashkent in May 2023. Restaurants also sell through **their own Telegram bots and phone lines**, and a local SaaS (**Delever**) exists to merge orders from several platforms into one system with POS/payment integration. I found no founder interviews describing day-to-day multi-tablet operations, packaging, peak times or courier splits.

### Cited Findings
**Aggregator landscape**
- The Competition Committee said Yandex Eats would control **87.5%** of the food-delivery aggregator market after buying part of Express24's assets. Yandex Eats and Express24 were declared dominant in Oct 2024 — [Gazeta.uz, 28.10.2024](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2024/10/28/yandex/); [uz24.uz](https://uz24.uz/ru/articles/monopol-10-28)
- **Uzum Tezkor** was declared a dominant digital platform on 02.04.2025. It operates in **19 cities** with **2,400+ partners** (cafés, restaurants, shops) and **~7,000 partner couriers**. Dominant platforms must announce changes to prices or terms **at least 15 days in advance** in media and on their sites, run antimonopoly compliance, and have their status reviewed every 3 years — [Gazeta.uz, 02.04.2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/04/02/uzum-tezkor/); [Kun.uz EN](https://kun.uz/en/news/2025/04/02/uzum-tezkor-named-dominant-digital-platform-in-delivery-market)
- Yandex Eats launched in Uzbekistan in **March 2023** with ~200–250 Tashkent restaurants (FeedUp, Bon!, Oqtepa Lavash, Yaponamama…). The company said that not every restaurant has its own delivery, which is why they join — [Afisha.uz, 07.03.2023](https://www.afisha.uz/ru/gorod/2023/03/07/yandex-eats)
- **Wolt** launched in Oct 2024 and announced its exit. New orders stopped on **5 March 2026**, and it has "no plans to return". An expert says ecosystem players (Uzum, Yandex) can subsidise delivery longer, and that demand is concentrated in Tashkent — [Spot.uz, 25.02.2026](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/02/25/wold-exit/); [Spot.uz, 02.03.2026](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/03/02/wolt-opinion/); [Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/01543038)
- Commission: one older vc.ru market overview estimated aggregator commissions at **~20–25%** and the market at ~$200M a year. **Undated / likely 2022–23, no per-platform breakdown** — [vc.ru (search snippet)](https://vc.ru/id851749/708992-vnutri-fudteha-uzbekistana-vedushie-igroki-i-obzor-rynka)

**Dark kitchens**
- On **12.05.2023**, Express24 launched Uzbekistan's first Dark Kitchen in the **Zarafshan shopping centre** (central Tashkent), chosen because it has the most orders. Express24 provides separate kitchen spaces to partner brands, which cook there and hand orders directly to couriers. Express24 analyses each venue's statistics to speed delivery. First tenant: "Moskovskaya pitstsa". Planned tenants: Osh Markazi, Testo, Shef Burger. Stated drivers: delivery demand grew sharply, many restaurants can't keep up with volume, and they want to expand delivery without high cost. No fees or revenue-split terms were disclosed — [Podrobno.uz, 12.05.2023](https://podrobno.uz/cat/obchestvo/v-uzbekistane-vpervye-zapustili-restorannyy-format-dark-kitchen/); [Spot.uz tag page](https://www.spot.uz/ru/tag/express24/). The current status of this hub after the Yandex–Express24 deal is **unknown**.

**Own channels (Telegram bot, phone)**
- Restaurants have long taken orders via **single phone number + Telegram bot** (e.g., Yapona Mama during the 2020 quarantine, and Paradise Food in 2020 by phone or Telegram bot) — [Afisha.uz, 2020](https://www.afisha.uz/ru/restaurants/2020/03/21/dostavka); [Afisha.uz, 2020 (advertorial)](https://www.afisha.uz/restaurants/2020/07/02/paradise-food) *(older, 2020)*
- **Delever** (Tashkent, founded 2021, ~17 staff) is a platform that "integrates all orders from various platforms", automates order intake and connects venues to payment systems and POS terminals. It offers API integration, SaaS subscription and white-label. It raised $100K from Aloqa Ventures (Jul 2024) — [Spot.uz, 10.07.2024](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2024/07/10/delever/); [digitalpool.gov.uz](https://digitalpool.gov.uz/en/company/delever) (snippet); [Daryo](https://daryo.uz/en/2024/07/11/uzbekistans-delever-secures-100000-in-venture-capital-from-aloqa-ventures/). Which aggregators it integrates with was not confirmed.
- A small Tashkent Uzbek-food delivery project said photographing every dish for an online menu was a budget problem — [workspace.ru case (snippet)](https://workspace.ru/cases/sayt-restorana-uzbekskoy-kuhni-i-sluzhby-dostavki-tashkent/)

**Home-based food sellers**
- The list of activities allowed for self-employed people (o'zini o'zi band qilgan shaxslar) is being cut **from 104 to 72 items from 01.01.2026**. Items removed include home popcorn and freezer ice-cream production — [Zamin.uz (search snippet)](https://zamin.uz/uz/iqtisodiyot/146949-ozini-ozini-band-qilgan-shaxslar-shugullanishi-mumkin-bolgan-faoliyat-turlari-qisqaradi.html). "Home processing and sale of milk/dairy products" was added from 01.11.2022 — [Kun.uz, 03.09.2022](https://www.kun.uz/news/2022/09/03/10-ta-faoliyat-turi-ozini-ozi-band-qilganlar-shugullanishi-mumkin-bolgan-ishlar-royxatiga-kiritildi). Whether **home cooking or baking for sale** is on the 2026 list was **not verified**.

### Inferences
- With Wolt gone and Express24 merged into Yandex, a POS in late 2026 realistically needs to take orders from **Yandex Eats, Uzum Tezkor, own Telegram bot/mini-app, phone/call-centre, and walk-in/self-pickup**. Express24 may still exist as a brand, but this needs checking.
- The existence of Delever (and similar order hubs) confirms that **merging orders from several channels** is a recognised pain in Tashkent. A POS can either integrate with such hubs or offer native aggregator connectors where APIs exist (Yandex Eats and Uzum Tezkor partner APIs are not publicly documented in my sources).
- Commission pressure (~20–25%, older estimate) plus dominant platforms suggests kitchens push customers to their **own Telegram bot**. Per-channel price lists (different prices or menus on aggregator vs own channel) and per-channel margin reports are likely needed.
- Home sellers' legal status is uncertain (the self-employed list keeps changing), so a product for them should not assume fiscal-receipt or registered-business features are available.

### Gaps
- **Current commission rates** for Yandex Eats and Uzum Tezkor for restaurants (2025–2026): not found.
- Whether aggregators allow **restaurant's own couriers** vs platform couriers, and self-pickup rules: not found.
- **Peak times**, packaging practices and costs, order volumes per dark kitchen, multi-tablet chaos: no founder interviews found in Uzbek or Russian.
- Status of the Express24 Zarafshan dark-kitchen hub after 2024: unknown.
- Whether Yandex Eats or Uzum Tezkor expose order APIs for POS integration (iiko/r_keeper/Poster/Delever connectors): not verified.
- How home-based sellers (Instagram/Telegram "uy ovqatlari", somsa, tort, manti) operate, how many there are, how they handle couriers (Yandex Go Delivery, taxi) and payments (Click/Payme transfers): no source found.
