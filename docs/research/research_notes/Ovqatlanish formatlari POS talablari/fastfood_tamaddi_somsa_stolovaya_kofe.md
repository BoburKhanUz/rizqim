# Counter-service food formats in Uzbekistan (fast food, tamaddixona, somsaxona, stolovaya/oshxona, qahvaxona) — operations as POS input

Research date: 2026-10-21. Scope: Uzbekistan, 2023–2026 practice. Every bullet under "Cited Findings" has a source; anything without a source is in "Inferences" or "Gaps". Several sources are sponsored articles ("На правах рекламы") or directory listings; this is flagged where it applies.

Overall source quality: the big chains (Oqtepa Lavash, Bellissimo, Max Way, EVOS, Feed Up) have public data from interviews, sponsored articles and their own sites. Small formats (tamaddixona, somsaxona, stolovaya) and coffee shops have almost no published operational data. For those formats, field interviews with owners are needed (see Gaps).

---

## Q1. How do the big Uzbek fast-food chains run orders (kiosks, queue screens, KDS, call centres, own apps), and which POS/software are they known to use?

### Takeaway
The big chains are omnichannel. Each runs its own app, website and Telegram bot plus a call centre or phone line, uses its own couriers, and lists on aggregators (Uzum Tezkor, Yandex Eats, Wolt). Kiosks are confirmed at only one chain: Feed Up, with Multikassa K2 kiosks in 12 branches. No source names which POS/ERP (iiko, R-Keeper, Jowi or in-house) EVOS, Max Way, Oqtepa or Bellissimo use. Bellissimo describes a single in-house-style IT platform (POS, warehouse, delivery, analytics) and tablets for the kitchen station.

### Cited Findings

**Oqtepa Lavash**
- About 100 restaurants (the 100th opened May 2026 at Seoul Mun, Tashkent), 11 regions, more than 2,000 employees, about 25,000 guests a day, its own meat production, logistics, delivery and "IT processes", FSSC 22000 certified. This is a sponsored article. — [Afisha.uz, 2026-05-25](https://www.afisha.uz/ru/restaurants/2026/05/25/oqtepa-lavash)

  - Speed target: 90% of orders cooked within 9 minutes, with delivery planned at 30 minutes. Currently 75% of orders are ready within 5 minutes. Pizza alone takes about 12 minutes and slows the line.
  - Peaks are lunch and dinner. Outside peaks, the space is used for the company's other brands.
  - Entry roles are assembly (сборка), cashier and hostess. Nobody may start work without passing an exam. Monthly unannounced inspections.
  - "An IT system provides online digital monitoring of all processes nationwide."
  - Branch profitability is about 40–48%, or about 30% after head-office costs. A full meal starts "от 16 тыс. сумов".

### Inferences
- The typical big-chain flow is: cashier or kiosk takes the order → number on the receipt → assembly line (сборщик) → handover at a pickup counter. The roles are confirmed (cashier, assembly, hostess), but no source describes the queue-number screens explicitly. A POS for this segment needs order numbers, a pickup or queue display, and kitchen routing to stations (tablet or KDS, as Bellissimo shows).
- Delivery channels are fragmented: own app, Telegram bot, call centre, website, and three aggregators (Uzum Tezkor, Yandex Eats, Wolt). A POS must merge all of them into one order queue and handle promo codes from each channel.
- Box or combo products are central for Max Way (Group Box, Super Box, Mini Box). Combos with substitutable components are a must-have unit of sale.
- Chains of 60–100 branches most likely run iiko, R-Keeper or in-house systems, but none of the named chains is confirmed. Do not claim a specific vendor.

### Gaps
- No public, verified statement of which POS/ERP EVOS, Max Way, Oqtepa, Bellissimo or Les Ailes use.
- No source on queue-number display screens or KDS hardware vendors at these chains.
- Delivery vs takeaway vs dine-in share for Uzbek branches is not published. The only figure is Bellissimo Kazakhstan's ~50/50.
- Call-centre share of orders: not found.
- hh.uz cashier ads did not show duty text. Cashier duty descriptions for Max Way, EVOS and Oqtepa remain unverified.

## Q2. How do stolovaya / oshxona (serving-line canteens) price a tray, and how do they operate?

### Takeaway
Almost no published Uzbek material describes stolovaya tray pricing. Indirect evidence points to per-portion pricing: average checks of 35–50k so'm, ready-lunch "sets" at 30–40k so'm, and equipment vendors selling serving lines with cash modules. Whether pricing is per item or by weight must be verified in field interviews.

### Cited Findings
- 2GIS shows average checks in Tashkent canteens of about 35,000–50,000 so'm; examples include Diet bistro at 50k and an "Эски-шахар" canteen near Chorsu at 40k. — [2GIS: Столовые Ташкента](https://2gis.uz/tashkent/search/%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B2%D1%8B%D0%B5/rubricId/166)
- Office-lunch delivery in Tashkent: full set 40,000 so'm, half set (no soup) 30,000 so'm, delivery 10,000 so'm (Telegram, undated). — [t.me/bistriyobed](https://t.me/s/bistriyobed)
- Office lunches advertised "from 35,000 so'm" on OLX. — [OLX.uz](https://www.olx.uz/tashkent/q-%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%B5%D0%B4%D1%8B-%D0%B2-%D0%BE%D1%84%D0%B8%D1%81/)
- Equipment for the format sold in Uzbekistan:
  - Serving lines (Abat models) with cash-desk modules; the Abat KK-70KM cash module is listed at 8.4 mln so'm. — [glotr.uz](https://glotr.uz/uz/oziq-ovqat-tarqatish-liniyalari/)
  - CAS portion scales at about 1.9–3.7 mln so'm. — [Prom.uz](https://www.prom.uz/uz/section/kukhonnye-vesy/)
- Kazakhstan analogue, not Uzbekistan: serving line → cashier at the end of the line → terminal. A combo is entered with one tap and salads are weighed at the cash desk. — [paloma365.kz](https://paloma365.kz/dlya-stolovoj)
- Traditional Chorsu food sellers (plov, norin, somsa, kebab) work from 4–7 a.m. Plov must be ready by 6:30 a.m. Customers "order by count", to eat there or take away. A kebab skewer cost 14,000 so'm (2022, older info). — [Gazeta.uz, 2022-10-11](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2022/10/11/chorsu/)

### Inferences
- Likely flow: the customer takes a tray, the server portions dishes, and the kassir at the end of the line rings up each dish (porsiya), with optional half portions (yarim porsiya) and bread or tea as add-ons. A POS needs a fast grid of 20–40 daily dishes, a daily menu (kunlik menyu) that changes, quick "tugadi" toggles, and optionally a weight (kg) item for salads. This is inferred from the Kazakhstan analogue and the equipment market. It is not observed in Uzbekistan.
- Plov-centre and oshxona dishes are cooked in large batches (qozon) early in the morning and sell out. Like somsa, they need batch-level stock and sell-out tracking.

### Gaps
- No Uzbek source on whether canteens price per portion or by weight, or on per-dish prices.
- No data on tray throughput at peak (people a minute at the lunch rush).
- No data on canteen POS usage (many may use a plain fiscal cash register or a notebook).

## Q3. How do somsaxonas handle batches, pre-orders and "tugadi"?

### Takeaway
Somsa production is batch-based: one tandir load at a time, sometimes "one tandir a day" for a village shop. Volumes range from a few hundred pieces a day (a small stall) to about 1,500 a day (a peak at a known Chorsu stall). No published source describes pre-order or sell-out handling; that must come from interviews.

### Cited Findings
- A family somsa business at Chorsu bazaar sells about 200–400 somsa a day now, and once sold about 1,500 a day.
  - Somsa is sold outdoors from large baskets wrapped in quilts.
  - The family bakes and the mother sold.
  - The current owner plans a restaurant serving only somsa (2022, older info).
  - [Gazeta.uz, 2022-10-11](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2022/10/11/chorsu/)
- A (fake) village somsaxona owner said: "Kuniga bir tandir somsa o'tkazaman va bundan katta daromad qilib bo'lmaydi". The mahalla already had three somsaxonas (2022 story, older info). — [Kun.uz, 2022-11-13](https://kun.uz/71245585?q=%2Fuz%2F71245585)
- Tandir market:
  - Small tandirs cost from 1.3 mln so'm and bakery (novvoy) tandirs 2.5–3 mln so'm.
  - Main buyers are bakeries and somsaxonas, including Korzinka, Beshqozon and Rayhon.
  - Orders come via Telegram and Instagram.
  - In season (summer) one maker builds 8–9 tandirs.
  - [Spot.uz, 2025-10-27](https://www.spot.uz/oz/2025/10/27/tandir/)
- Somsa is baked in a ground tandir or gas oven; a common method sticks four somsas together by their sides before baking. — [uz.wikipedia: Somsa](https://uz.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somsa)
- A Telegram channel "OSHXONA 20 TURDAN ORTIQ SOMSALAR" advertises 20+ somsa types, which shows a multi-SKU somsa assortment. — [t.me/oshxonasomsamarkazi](https://t.me/s/oshxonasomsamarkazi?before=2863)
- Unverified YouTube titles claim a Namangan somsa centre sells 1,000 a day (search summary only, not opened). — no reliable source

### Inferences
- POS needs for this format:
  - Unit of sale is dona (piece), often with a quantity multiplier (e.g. 10 dona to take away).
  - Several types: go'shtli, kartoshkali, qovoqli, tovuqli and others.
  - Batch (partiya / "bir tandir") quantity entry, so remaining stock decrements and sales auto-stop with "tugadi" at zero.
  - Expected time of the next batch ("keyingi partiya 20 daqiqada").
  - Pre-orders for large counts (for events or offices), with a deposit.
  - Mostly cash and QR; few or no modifiers.
- Peak is likely morning to lunch, given early-morning production habits of bazaar food sellers. This is not confirmed for somsa specifically.

### Gaps
- Somsa per tandir load, loads per day and the time per load: not found in any published source.
- Pre-order practice (phone, Telegram) and how shops communicate "tugadi": not found.
- Prices per somsa in 2025–2026: not found in the sources reviewed.

## Q4. How do coffee shops (qahvaxona) handle modifiers (milk type, size, syrups) and loyalty?

### Takeaway
Tashkent coffee shops offer alternative milks, rafs and syrups, so a POS needs modifier groups. Loyalty is app-based and cashback-style: B&B up to 6% cashback through its app, Bellissimo-style coins in the wider food sector. The Bon! chain has about 24 locations and accepts app and QR payment. No published menu with sizes and modifier prices for a Tashkent chain was found.

### Cited Findings
- Alternative milk in Tashkent:
  - Beanberry serves cappuccino and latte on plant milk, caramel macchiato, flat white and vanilla raf.
  - Bon! has espresso on coconut milk.
  - ЧайКоф makes any drink with soy or coconut milk.
  - B&B Coffee House offers drinks on alternative milk.
  - [ANONS.uz](https://www.anons.uz/ru/news/kofeyni-tashkenta)
- Bon! was described as a French-style coffee chain with 24 locations in Tashkent; the article is about 2 years old. — [weproject.media](https://weproject.media/articles/detail/modnye-kofeyni-tashkenta-kotorye-stoit-posetit/)
- The Bon! 2GIS card lists payment by app and QR code. — [2GIS: Bon!](https://2gis.uz/tashkent/firm/70000001036617316)
- B&B Coffee House (Tashkent) advertises cashback of up to 6% on orders through its app (Facebook page, via search summary). — [B&B Coffee House Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/bnb.uz/)
- Oqtepa Lavash operates coffee shops alongside its lavash restaurants (Dec 2024). — [Spot.uz tag page](https://www.spot.uz/ru/tag/oqtepa%20lavash/)
- Typical checks on 2GIS start at about 35,000 so'm (Specialty coffee alliance). Breakfast with coffee at BnB is about 150,000 so'm per person (tourist blog, older than 200 days). — [2GIS: Кофейни](https://2gis.uz/tashkent/search/%D0%9A%D0%BE%D1%84%D0%B5%D0%B9%D0%BD%D0%B8/rubricId/162)
- Moona Cafe lists an "инди латте" at 55,000 so'm. — [moona.shop](https://moona.shop/cafe/)

### Inferences
- Modifier groups a coffee POS needs:
  - Size (S/M/L or 250/350/450 ml; the Russian-chain pattern at [Coffeeway](https://coffeeway.ru/menu/tproduct/606898491-793435893811-raf) is a benchmark, not Uzbekistan).
  - Milk (cow, oat, coconut, soy, with a surcharge).
  - Syrups (multi-select, paid per pump or portion).
  - Extra shot, temperature or ice.
  - Each modifier must reach the barista ticket or screen.
- Loyalty is percentage cashback in an app or by QR at the till, the same pattern as B&B and Bellissimo. Stamp cards ("10th coffee free") are common elsewhere but were not confirmed in Uzbekistan.

### Gaps
- No Tashkent chain menu with size, milk and syrup surcharges was found.
- No data on coffee-shop order volumes at the morning peak, or on the takeaway share.
- Loyalty mechanics at Bon!, Tim's, Coffee Hub and Safia were not found.

## Q5. Typical average checks and order volumes at peak

### Takeaway
Fast-food checks are about 30–60k so'm (Max Way's own figure), with Oqtepa entry meals from 16k so'm and fixed-price items at 10k, 15k and 20k so'm. Canteens are about 35–50k so'm on 2GIS. Volume points: Oqtepa serves about 25k guests a day across about 100 branches (about 250 a branch on average). Bellissimo makes 500k+ pizzas a month in Uzbekistan. Bellissimo Kazakhstan has 150–160 orders a branch a day. No peak-hour throughput figures were found.

### Cited Findings
- Max Way average check: 30–60k so'm. — [maxway.uz](https://maxway.uz/en/about)
- Oqtepa: full meal from 16k so'm; 25k guests a day; lavash in 65–70% of checks. — [Spot.uz, 2025-10-29](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2025/10/29/oqtepa-lavash/)
- Oqtepa "Смешные цены" fixed-price items at 10k, 15k and 20k so'm (May 2025). — [Afisha.uz](https://www.afisha.uz/ru/restaurants/2025/05/23/oqtepa-lavash)
- Oqtepa promo threshold of 79k so'm for delivery (Oct 2024). — [Afisha.uz](https://www.afisha.uz/ru/gorod/2024/10/29/oqtepa-lavash)
- Bellissimo: more than 500k pizzas a month in Uzbekistan across almost 100 restaurants. Kazakhstan branches: 150–160 orders a day, average check $11–12, one branch with more than 100 orders by 4 p.m. — [Spot.uz, 2026-08-27](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/08/27/bellissimo-kz/)
- Bellissimo promo code example: orders from 100,000 so'm. — [Gazeta.uz](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2024/12/24/bellissimo-pizza/)
- Canteen checks of 35–50k so'm. — [2GIS](https://2gis.uz/tashkent/search/%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B2%D1%8B%D0%B5/rubricId/166)
- Somsa stall: 200–400 pieces a day (2022). — [Gazeta.uz](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2022/10/11/chorsu/)
- EVOS Express: about 3 minutes from order to food (sponsored). — [Afisha.uz](https://www.afisha.uz/ru/restaurants/2025/06/24/evos)

### Inferences
- Bellissimo Uzbekistan: 500k pizzas ÷ about 100 branches ÷ 30 days ≈ 170 pizzas a branch a day (not orders; one order can contain several pizzas).
- Oqtepa: 25k guests ÷ about 100 branches ≈ 250 guests a branch a day on average. If 40–50% of these arrive in the lunch and dinner peaks (an assumption), one cashier must handle roughly one order a minute at peak. The POS checkout must therefore take about 30 seconds or less per order.

### Gaps
- Peak-hour order counts and orders per hour per cashier: not published for any Uzbek chain.
- Average check for EVOS, Oqtepa, Feed Up and Les Ailes: not published.

## Q6. Payment mix, fiscal and regulatory context relevant to POS (all formats)

### Takeaway
Payments are cash, Uzcard/Humo cards and QR through Click, Payme, Uzum and Rahmat. Since 1 July 2026 legal entities must also accept the state UzQR, which carries a 0.65% fee paid by the seller. Cash is restricted for alcohol and tobacco. No source gives the cash vs card vs QR share for catering.

### Cited Findings
- From 1 July 2026, legal entities in trade and services must accept payment through the single UzQR code, displayed at the cash desk.
  - Fee: 0.65% paid by the seller. By comparison, terminal payments cost about 0.20%, and bank rates vary from 0.25% to 0.65%.
  - Existing Payme, Click, Uzum and TezQR codes may stay. Card terminal and cash acceptance remain mandatory.
  - Individual entrepreneurs and self-employed have needed a special QR since 1 January; currently only Paynet Xolis offers one. Fines: 5 mln so'm for a missing code, 20 mln so'm for using someone else's.
  - [Kun.uz, 2026-07-03](https://kun.uz/ru/news/2026/07/03/v-uzbekistane-zapushchen-uzqr-otvety-na-glavnyye-voprosy-ce5d99)
- From 1 April 2026, alcohol and tobacco can only be paid non-cash.
  - Guests pay by card or by QR through Rahmat, Click or Payme. The restaurateur says QR or Rahmat fees cost about 2% of turnover.
  - Restaurants must split one table into separate checks for food and for alcohol or tobacco ("double bookkeeping").
  - Alcohol marking must be scanned and the data goes to the tax authority. Loan costs reach 28%.
  - [Spot.uz, 2026-04-07](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/04/07/alco-ban/)
- The government target is 75% non-cash payments by 2030. Cash for alcohol and tobacco in cafes was reportedly allowed again from 1 June 2026; sources conflict on the date. — [Yandex Travel](https://travel.yandex.ru/journal/kak-teper-platyat-turisty-v-uzbekistane/); [Gazeta.uz, 2026-04-23](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/04/23/alcohol/)
- In ordinary cafes people often still pay cash. — [travelto.ru](https://travelto.ru/blog/kak-platit-v-uzbekistane/)

### Inferences
- POS must support:
  - Split tender (cash plus card plus QR on one check).
  - Separate checks by product category (alcohol and tobacco).
  - Fiscal receipts (the online fiscal module / OFD requirement was not researched here).
  - Showing a payment QR (UzQR, Click, Payme) on the customer display.
  - Reconciling QR payments per provider at shift close.

### Gaps
- Cash / card / QR share in fast food, canteens and somsaxonas: no data.

## Q7. Tamaddixona (small snack places): operations

### Takeaway
No dedicated published source on tamaddixona operations was found. Only adjacent evidence exists: bazaar sellers selling "by count", for eating in or taking away, and very early production starts.

### Cited Findings
- Bazaar food sellers start work at 4–5 a.m., have the main dish ready about 10 a.m., and customers "order by count, either to eat there or take away" (2022). — [Gazeta.uz, 2022-10-11](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2022/10/11/chorsu/)

### Inferences
- A tamaddixona likely mixes formats: somsa or pirojki by the piece, hot-dogs or shawarma made to order, and tea or drinks. The owner is often also the cashier, so the POS must be usable on a phone or tablet, work with a single user, and cope with offline use.

- No source on tamaddixona staffing, equipment, payment mix or software use. This needs field interviews.
