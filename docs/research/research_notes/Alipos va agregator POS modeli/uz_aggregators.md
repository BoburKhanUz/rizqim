# Food delivery aggregators in Uzbekistan (2024–2026): commission, terms and merchant tools

Scope note: this extends the aggregator section of `docs/research/reports/Ovqatlanish tarmoqlari uchun POS talablari.md` (lines ~230–236). That report already has: Yandex bought Express24 assets (Oct 2024) and holds 87.5%; Uzum Tezkor was declared dominant (Apr 2025, 2,400+ partners, 19 cities); the 15-day notice rule; Wolt exited 5 Mar 2026; the 20–30% (Busoft/zira.uz) and 30–35% (Delever) commission estimates; Yandex KG integration thresholds; Delever 400+ restaurants. Those facts are not repeated below unless new detail was found.
Labels: **[CONFIRMED]** primary or official source; **[PRESS]** independent media; **[VENDOR]** claim by the company or a software seller; **[FOREIGN]** another country, not verified for UZ; **[OLD]** before 2025.
Research date: 24 Oct 2026.

## 1. Which aggregators operate in Uzbekistan in 2026

### Takeaway
By late 2026 the market has two players: **Yandex Eats** (Yandex Go app, with Express24's restaurant base merged in) and **Uzum Tezkor** (inside the Uzum ecosystem, widest regional coverage). Wolt left on 5 March 2026. No evidence was found that Glovo ever launched in Uzbekistan. Big chains also run their own Telegram bots, and software vendors (Delever, Mison, laCafe) sell "own channel" ordering to restaurants. No separate food marketplace inside Click or Payme was found in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- **Yandex Eats** launched in Tashkent in March 2023 with 250+ restaurants. Yandex had no courier fleet of its own: orders were carried by partner logistics firms' couriers and by restaurants' own couriers [OLD, PRESS] — [UzDaily](https://www.uzdaily.uz/en/yandex-eats-service-launched-in-uzbekistan/); [spot.uz, 07.03.2023](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2023/03/07/yandex-eats/)
- On 25 Oct 2024 Yandex Uzbekistan bought part of Express24's assets. The companies said Yandex Go's Eats and Express24 would move to **one technology platform**, and Express24 partner restaurants would get a wider delivery zone and the new platform's promotion tools [PRESS, company claim] — [Kun.uz, 25.10.2024](https://kun.uz/news/2024/10/25/yandex-uzbekistan-express24ning-bir-qismini-sotib-oladi); [Gazeta.uz, 25.10.2024](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2024/10/25/yandex-express24/)
- The Competition Committee named YandexGo UB LLC, Yandex Eats LLC JV and Express24 APP LLC dominant (announced 26.10.2024). They must run an antitrust compliance system and are under ongoing monitoring [PRESS] — [Kun.uz EN, 26.10.2024](https://kun.uz/en/news/2024/10/26/uzbekistans-food-delivery-market-deemed-monopolized-by-yandex-eats-and-express24)
- Yandex's other food-related products in UZ: Yandex Lavka (grocery from its own dark stores, launched 2024, also sells ready food, delivery fee 10–10,000 soums) and **Yandex Ultima Go** (premium restaurant delivery inside Yandex Go, 2024). The same article calls Yandex Eats "Yandex.Taom" [PRESS] — [Zira.uz, 17.11.2025](https://zira.uz/ru/2025/11/17/kak-segodnya-rabotaet-dostavka-v-uzbek/)
- **Uzum Tezkor** launched on 15 May 2023. In May 2026 (3rd anniversary) it was in **26 cities** with **7,000+ couriers**, and called itself the largest delivery service by geographic reach [VENDOR: sponsored article] — [Gazeta.uz, 21.05.2026 (advertorial)](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/05/21/uzum-tezkor/)
- New in **Sept 2026**: Uzum Tezkor sells **courier-only delivery for orders restaurants take on their own channels** (phone, Telegram, own app, social media). The restaurant picks "Uzum Tezkor delivery", a courier task is created automatically, and the customer gets a tracking link and Uzum Tezkor support. Available in 13 cities. **No price was published.** The article only says pricing is "transparent" and does not change with weather or traffic [VENDOR: advertorial] — [spot.uz, 14.09.2026](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/09/14/uzum-tezkor/)
- **Wolt** entered in 2024 (Tashkent, then Samarkand). By June 2025 it listed about **1,400 restaurants and 200+ shops** in Tashkent. It closed on **5 March 2026** as part of a DoorDash portfolio review that also covered Qatar, Singapore and Japan [PRESS] — [Kun.uz EN, 25.02.2026](https://kun.uz/en/news/2026/02/25/wolt-announces-exit-from-uzbekistan-market); [UzDaily](https://www.uzdaily.uz/en/wolt-delivery-service-to-cease-operations-in-uzbekistan-from-5-march-2026/); [Kursiv, 25.02.2026](https://uz.kursiv.media/en/2026-02-25/wolt-delivery-service-shuts-down-in-uzbekistan-from-march-5/)
- In 2025 Wolt positioned itself on restaurants and small family establishments and was widening its Tashkent zone [PRESS] — [Zira.uz, 17.11.2025](https://zira.uz/ru/2025/11/17/kak-segodnya-rabotaet-dostavka-v-uzbek/)
- **Glovo:** no report of a launch in Uzbekistan was found. Glovo operates in Kyrgyzstan (since 2020) and Kazakhstan (since 2019) [PRESS, FOREIGN] — [economist.kg, 23.07.2024](https://economist.kg/novosti-kompanii/2024/07/23/v-tiekushchiem-ghodu-glovo-planiruiet-vlozhit-eu6-mln-na-razvitiie-bizniesa-soosnovatiel-kompanii-sasha-misho/)
- Smaller or local services named in 2025: **Noor** and **Mars Express** (Samarkand only, no details), plus **Korzinka Go** (grocery) [PRESS] — [Zira.uz, 17.11.2025](https://zira.uz/ru/2025/11/17/kak-segodnya-rabotaet-dostavka-v-uzbek/). **Broniboy** was a small general-delivery player in 2023 [OLD] — [vc.ru, 15.06.2023](https://vc.ru/id851749/708992-vnutri-fudteha-uzbekistana-vedushie-igroki-i-obzor-rynka)
- POS vendor Mison lists three aggregators it connects: "Yandex Eda, Uzum Tezkor and **Tez Chakana**" (2026) [VENDOR] — [mison.uz](https://mison.uz/ru/). A web search found nothing else about "Tez Chakana", so what it is remains unverified.
- **Own-channel software for restaurants:** Mison sells four "0% commission" channels (call center, Telegram bot, website, QR menu) [VENDOR] — [mison.uz](https://mison.uz/ru/). laCafe sells online ordering and delivery for cafes and lists iiko, Jowi, rKeeper and Poster integrations [VENDOR] — [lacafe.uz](https://lacafe.uz/en/). Uzum Tezkor itself launched a Telegram bot for ordering food (Dec 2023) [OLD] — [Kursiv, 19.12.2023](https://uz.kursiv.media/2023-12-19/uzum-tezkor-zapustil-telegram-bota-dlya-zakaza-edy/)

### Inferences
- For Rizqim the real competition is a **duopoly (Yandex Eats and Uzum Tezkor)**, both formally "dominant" and both tied to a super-app (Yandex Go; Uzum Market/Bank/Nasiya). Wolt's exit removed the one player that targeted small family places, which leaves a gap Rizqim can aim at.
- Uzum Tezkor's Sept 2026 "courier for your own orders" product competes directly with Rizqim's delivery layer. It also lets restaurants that sell through their own Telegram channel use Uzum couriers, so Rizqim should either connect to it or offer a better courier price.
- Most "Telegram marketplace" competition comes from POS or SaaS vendors that sell each restaurant its own bot (Mison, Delever, laCafe), not from a shared marketplace. No shared, multi-restaurant Telegram marketplace with POS was found (this matches the earlier report).

### Gaps
- Click and Payme super-app food sections: not researched in depth. No evidence found either way of an active restaurant marketplace in Click or Payme in 2026.
- No dedicated choyxona or osh delivery aggregator was found (only chains' own bots).
- What "Tez Chakana" is could not be identified.
- After its merger with Express24, does Yandex still run the Express24 brand or app separately in 2026? No 2026 source found.

## 2. Commission, fees, payouts, exclusivity and restaurant sentiment

### Takeaway
**Neither Yandex Eats nor Uzum Tezkor publishes a commission rate for Uzbekistan.** Every number in the public domain is a third-party estimate: **20–25%** (vc.ru, 2023), **20–30%** (Busoft integrator via Zira.uz, Nov 2025), **30–35%** (Delever), and a "−30% per check" graphic (Mison 2026). These suggest roughly **20–30% when the aggregator delivers**. No onboarding fee is advertised. Uzum says payouts are "regular" with documents in Didox. No exclusivity, marketing-fee or payout-cycle terms were found.

### Cited Findings
- "Service commissions on the market are 20–25%" (Uzbekistan, June 2023) [OLD, PRESS/blog] — [vc.ru, 15.06.2023](https://vc.ru/id851749/708992-vnutri-fudteha-uzbekistana-vedushie-igroki-i-obzor-rynka)
- Busoft (r_keeper integrator): aggregator commission is **20–30%** of the order, plus packaging of about **3,000–5,000 soums per order**. It also warns that discounts and promos eat margin and that customer loyalty shifts to the aggregator [VENDOR via PRESS] — [Zira.uz, 17.11.2025](https://zira.uz/ru/2025/11/17/kak-segodnya-rabotaet-dostavka-v-uzbek/)
- Mison's 2026 page shows a **"−30%"** platform cut per check and says "every third order you give to the platform". It sets a **separate markup on aggregator menus** so the commission is covered without changing dine-in prices. It does not name the actual rate of any aggregator [VENDOR] — [mison.uz](https://mison.uz/ru/)
- **Menu-price markup vs. price parity:** Uzum Tezkor runs a "price guarantee" badge, meaning most dishes cost the same as in the restaurant [VENDOR/PRESS] — [repost.uz](https://repost.uz/garantiya-ot-uzum). Restaurants that mark up aggregator menus (as Mison recommends) cannot get that badge. This is inferred from the two sources, not confirmed.
- **Uzum Tezkor partner page:** onboarding through a Telegram bot (@UzumTezkorbot_bot), "registration takes no more than 5 minutes". It promises "regular payments and transparent reports", with all financial documents in **Didox** (e-document system). Customers pay by card, cash, **Uzum Bank** or **Uzum Nasiya** (BNPL). Partner restaurants also show in the Tezkor section of **Uzum Market**. No commission, fee or payout period is shown on the page; it links to an offer document that was not retrieved [VENDOR] — [promo.tezkor.com/partners/uz](https://promo.tezkor.com/partners/uz)
- Uzum Market's seller offer (the sister marketplace, not Tezkor) is an intermediary agreement with a **percentage reward on the seller's proceeds**. Category rates are about 3–25% (one guide) or 3–35% (another) [VENDOR, not Tezkor] — [seller.uzum.uz offer](https://seller.uzum.uz/seller/agreement/uz/); [moysklad.uz](https://www.moysklad.uz/uz/poleznoe/marketplejsy/kak-prodavat-na-uzum/). The Tezkor offer probably uses the same intermediary model with a percentage reward, but this is an inference.
- **Regulatory protection for restaurants:** as dominant platforms, Yandex Eats, Express24 and Uzum Tezkor must announce price or term changes in the media and on their website **at least 15 days in advance**. Dominance criteria (from 1 Aug 2024): revenue above 37.5 bn soums, or ≥50,000 monthly active end users, or ≥3,000 monthly active business users, or >40% market share [PRESS] — [Gazeta.uz, 02.04.2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/04/02/uzum-tezkor/)
- Foreign benchmark (Kazakhstan): Yandex Eats **20–35%**, Wolt **25–30%**. The rate depends on city, average check, format and whose couriers deliver [FOREIGN, bank blog] — [alataucitybank.kz](https://alataucitybank.kz/business/osim/articles/kak-zavedeniyu-vybrat-agregator-dostavki)
- Foreign benchmark (Russia): Yandex Eda partners get fines for courier delays and taste complaints, and can be disconnected after enough penalty points. In 2022 Yandex set up an industry commission to hear restaurant disputes [FOREIGN] — [Vedomosti, 05.08.2022](https://www.vedomosti.ru/business/news/2022/08/05/934730-yandeks-eda-sozdast-komissiyu-dlya-razresheniya-sporov); [RB.ru](https://rb.ru/news/yaeda-comission1/). No such dispute body was found for Uzbekistan.
- Restaurants' reliance on delivery: partners say delivery is **20–30%** of orders (Uzum Tezkor, July 2024) [VENDOR] — [spot.uz, 22.07.2024](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2024/07/22/uzum-market/), "up to 40%" (Sept 2024) [VENDOR] — [Gazeta.uz, 06.09.2024](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2024/09/06/uzum-tezkor/), and **30–50%** (May 2026) [VENDOR] — [Gazeta.uz advertorial, 21.05.2026](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/05/21/uzum-tezkor/)

### Inferences
- A working assumption for positioning is **"aggregators take about 20–30% (up to 35%)"**, always labelled as an estimate. Rizqim's commission plus POS bundle has a clear price anchor if its total cost to the restaurant (commission plus subscription) stays well below about 20% of delivery GMV.
- Restaurants already respond to commissions with dual pricing (marked-up aggregator menus). Rizqim's POS should support **per-channel prices** and a **per-channel margin report** so owners can see what each channel really earns them.
- The 15-day notice rule means aggregators cannot change commissions overnight. If Rizqim crosses a dominance threshold (3,000 monthly active business users), the same duty would apply to it.

### Gaps
- **No official commission rate** for Yandex Eats UZ or Uzum Tezkor (aggregator delivery vs. own-courier vs. pickup) was found. The Yandex partner page (eda.yandex.uz/partner) returned a captcha, and the Tezkor offer document was not found. Best next step: ask 2–3 partner restaurants or request the offers directly.
- No published payout cycle (weekly or bi-weekly), onboarding fee, marketing or "promotion" fee schedule, or exclusivity clause.
- No Uzbek press report, Telegram-channel thread or Competition Committee case specifically about restaurants complaining over aggregator commissions in 2024–2026 was found in this pass. This is a real gap: "restaurant sentiment" in UZ remains anecdotal (integrator and vendor claims only).
- Price of Uzum Tezkor's Sept 2026 courier-only service: not published.

## 3. Merchant tools: order apps, POS integrations, stop-lists and fiscal receipts

### Takeaway
Both aggregators let restaurants take orders in a web or app cabinet on any device (Yandex "Vendor"; Uzum's partner platform). Direct POS integration covers mainly **iiko, R-Keeper and JOWi** (Uzum lists all three; Yandex had JOWi in 2023 and iiko/r_keeper abroad). Poster, Clopos, Alipos and Mison connect through their own or third-party connectors. Where there is no integration, staff **re-key orders into the till by hand**. Who issues the fiscal receipt for aggregator orders in UZ is still not documented publicly.

### Cited Findings
- Uzum Tezkor partner page: the partner platform works on **any device (computer, phone, tablet)**, with no dedicated hardware. Integrations listed: **iiko, R-Keeper, JOWi** [VENDOR] — [promo.tezkor.com/partners/uz](https://promo.tezkor.com/partners/uz)
- Uzum Tezkor and iiko strategic partnership (Aug 2023): end-to-end order flow from receipt to hand-over, said to cut delivery time by 5–7 minutes. About 150 Tashkent venues (about 1 in 3 partners) used iiko then [OLD, PRESS] — [Daryo.uz, 07.08.2023](https://daryo.uz/2023/08/07/bizning-vazifamiz-taomlarni-issiqligicha-olib-kelish-uzum-tezkor-yetkazib-berish-servisi-iiko/)
- Yandex Eats integration in UZ: JOWi was integrated in May 2023 (already in the base report). In Russia and Kyrgyzstan the routes are the iiko module (iikoWeb external menu and order source), the r_keeper Delivery module (separate licence), POS vendors with a ready Yandex API connector, a custom API, or **no integration with orders in the Vendor cabinet** [FOREIGN] — [agregatory.pro](https://agregatory.pro/blog/yandex-eda-integratsiya-iiko); [Yandex Eda KG](https://yandex.ru/project/eda/kg/rest_faq_integration)
- Manual workflow when there is no integration: an Osh (Kyrgyzstan) restaurant read orders in the **Yandex Vendor app** and re-entered them in iiko, using a separate delivery menu to keep prices right [FOREIGN] — [iweb.kg case](https://iweb.kg/en/cases/iiko-eden-taste)
- Other POS vendors that sell aggregator connectivity in UZ: **Mison** (Yandex Eda, Uzum Tezkor, Tez Chakana set up "in 3 days" only on its "Full" plan at **690,000 soums/month per outlet**; the "Start" plan at 390,000 soums has none) [VENDOR] — [mison.uz](https://mison.uz/ru/). **Clopos** takes Yandex Go, Uzum and Wolt orders (from the base report) [VENDOR]. **laCafe** lists iiko/Jowi/rKeeper/Poster [VENDOR] — [lacafe.uz](https://lacafe.uz/en/).
- Menu and stop-list sync: Mison says it "adapts your menu to the platform's requirements" and creates the aggregator account [VENDOR] — [mison.uz](https://mison.uz/ru/). No UZ source describes automatic stop-list sync to Uzum or Yandex. With iiko it is part of the standard iiko/Yandex module abroad [FOREIGN] — [agregatory.pro](https://agregatory.pro/blog/yandex-eda-integratsiya-iiko)
- Fiscal receipt: a Russian description of the Yandex Eda model says the aggregator acts as an agent, keeps its commission, and **the restaurant prints the receipt** [FOREIGN, secondary] — [surf.ru](https://surf.ru/prilozhenie-kak-yandex-eda/). For UZ, the base report already notes that payment systems may issue an e-receipt with the restaurant's TIN as principal (komitent), and that aggregator receipt practice is not officially explained.
- Uzum keeps partner financial documents in Didox (e-invoices and acts), which points to an intermediary or agency settlement model [VENDOR] — [promo.tezkor.com/partners/uz](https://promo.tezkor.com/partners/uz)

### Inferences
- The integration bar is low for small places: both aggregators work through a browser or phone cabinet. The pain is **double entry** (aggregator cabinet → POS) and **menu or stop-list drift**. A Rizqim POS that pulls Yandex and Uzum orders into one queue, alongside Rizqim's own Telegram orders, solves a real problem. Today that is sold only in mid-to-high plans (Mison 690k/month) or through iiko/R-Keeper, which choyxonas and osh centres do not run.
- Uzum's official integration list (iiko, R-Keeper, JOWi) does not include new local POSes. Rizqim would need a partner API from Uzum or Yandex, or would start by **semi-manual import** (cabinet → POS with an external number and commission %), as the base report proposes.
- Fiscal receipt for aggregator orders is a selling point Rizqim can only use after a tax adviser confirms who must issue it. The likely answer is that the restaurant issues it (agency model) unless the payment provider issues a principal-marked e-receipt.

### Gaps
- Whether Yandex Eats UZ supports iiko or r_keeper API integration today, and its UZ eligibility thresholds (the KG page says rating ≥4.6 and ≥8 outlets or >7 orders/day).
- Whether Alipos, Poster or Clopos have **official** Uzum Tezkor or Yandex Eats UZ integrations (only vendor claims).
- An official State Tax Committee position on who prints the fiscal receipt for aggregator orders (prepaid in-app vs. cash to courier).

## 4. Market shares, order volumes and partner counts

### Takeaway
The only official share figure is **87.5%** for Yandex Eats plus Express24 (Competition Committee, Oct 2024, based on end-2023 data). Partner counts are all company figures: Uzum Tezkor grew from 1,000+ to **2,600 partners in 25 cities** (Apr 2025) and to **26 cities and 7,000+ couriers** (May 2026). Wolt had about 1,400 restaurants before it left. No 2025–2026 restaurant count was found for Yandex Eats.

### Cited Findings
- Yandex Eats controls **87.5%** of the ready-food aggregator market after buying Express24 assets (Competition Committee) [PRESS, regulator figure] — [Gazeta.uz, 29.10.2024](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2024/10/29/express/)
- Uzum Tezkor partner counts [all VENDOR]:
  - 1,600+ cafes, restaurants and shops in 8 cities (Sept 2024) — [Gazeta.uz, 06.09.2024](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2024/09/06/uzum-tezkor/)
  - 2,400+ partners, 19 cities, about 7,000 couriers (2 Apr 2025) — [Gazeta.uz, 02.04.2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/04/02/uzum-tezkor/)
  - 2,600 partners, 25 cities, 7,000 couriers (18 Apr 2025) — [spot.uz, 18.04.2025](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2025/04/18/uzum-tezkor/)
  - 26 cities, 7,000+ couriers (May 2026); no restaurant count — [Gazeta.uz advertorial, 21.05.2026](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/05/21/uzum-tezkor/)
  - The partner page still says "1,000+ partners" (stale, undated) — [promo.tezkor.com](https://promo.tezkor.com/partners/uz)
- Uzum Tezkor growth (May 2026, company): year-on-year turnover about **2x**; retail more than 3x; regional turnover **2.5x in 2025**; regions are about **1/3 of business**; average delivery 30–40 min in large cities; order record in the first week of May 2026 [VENDOR] — [Gazeta.uz advertorial, 21.05.2026](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/05/21/uzum-tezkor/)
- Yandex Eats restaurant counts: 250+ at launch (Mar 2023) — [UzDaily](https://www.uzdaily.uz/en/yandex-eats-service-launched-in-uzbekistan/); 200+ in Tashkent (June 2023) — [vc.ru](https://vc.ru/id851749/708992-vnutri-fudteha-uzbekistana-vedushie-igroki-i-obzor-rynka); "800+ local and chain partners" (undated blog) — [sharh.commeta.uz](https://sharh.commeta.uz/en/blog/yetkazib-berish-xizmatlari-jangi-uzum-tezkor-va-yandex-eatsga-qarshi?isMobile=true) [all OLD or undated]
- Express24 before the merger: "700+ restaurants, 5 cities" (undated Beeline promo) [VENDOR, undated] — [beeline.uz](https://beeline.uz/ru/events/beeline-club/express24-darit-vsem-uchastnikam-beeline-club-skidku-20000-sum-na-pervyj-zakaz)
- Wolt: about 1,400 restaurants and 200+ shops in Tashkent (June 2025) [PRESS] — [Kun.uz EN, 25.02.2026](https://kun.uz/en/news/2026/02/25/wolt-announces-exit-from-uzbekistan-market)
- Estimated delivery market turnover: **up to $200 m/year** (2023 estimate) [OLD, blog] — [vc.ru, 15.06.2023](https://vc.ru/id851749/708992-vnutri-fudteha-uzbekistana-vedushie-igroki-i-obzor-rynka)

### Inferences
- Combined, the two aggregators list somewhere in the low thousands of food places, concentrated in chains and city cafes. Against the tens of thousands of catering outlets in UZ (see the base report's ~64% "no software" segment), most choyxonas and osh centres are **not on any aggregator**. That is Rizqim's white space.
- Wolt's roughly 1,400 Tashkent restaurants lost a channel in March 2026. Many smaller ones may not have moved to Yandex or Uzum, which makes them a natural onboarding target (inference, not verified).

### Gaps
- Uzum Tezkor's market share after Wolt's exit, and Yandex Eats' 2025–2026 restaurant count and order volumes: not published.
- No independent order-volume data (e.g. from the Competition Committee or analytics firms) for 2025–2026.
- No raqobat.gov.uz decision text was retrieved directly. Facts come from press reports of the decisions.
